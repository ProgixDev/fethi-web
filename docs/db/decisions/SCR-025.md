# SCR-025 — Held seller proceeds for card purchases

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-08-23
Author: Codex (fethi-mobile issue #35)
Migration: `20260823212133_held_seller_proceeds.sql`

## Decision

Card purchases use a Stripe platform charge and a later, linked Connect
Transfer. MyStreet records the captured amount in `held_seller_proceeds` and
does not transfer seller proceeds at capture time. This is a payment-flow hold,
not a legal escrow arrangement.

The authoritative #30 Order snapshot remains the only monetary source:

- the buyer is charged `orders.amount_cents` in EUR;
- the seller's own commission is `orders.seller_fee_cents`;
- the transfer is that seller net less any outstanding handoff-sale
  receivables, never a client-supplied amount.

An authenticated buyer's pickup confirmation schedules release. The transfer is
available immediately below €500 and after 48 hours for €500 or more. A hold
with no buyer confirmation for seven calendar days becomes `REVIEW_REQUIRED`:
no money moves automatically and staff must release or refund it. A Stripe
refund, dispute, or cancellation terminally prevents release.

## Consequences

- Sellers must be Didit `VERIFIED` and have an enabled Stripe Connect account
  before a card PaymentIntent can be created.
- Stripe webhooks are the source of truth for payment/refund/dispute state and
  create exactly one hold per successful charge.
- Transfers use Stripe and database idempotency keys, and are tied to the
  original Stripe Charge with `source_transaction`.
- Separate charges make MyStreet responsible for refund/dispute liabilities.
  If a transfer was already made, the staff refund path reverses it before
  issuing the buyer refund.
- The scheduled reconciler must be invoked at least every five minutes with its
  service secret to release due holds and mark seven-day reviews.
